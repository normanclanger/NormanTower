The final of the National 12 Bell Striking Contest is being held at St Edmundsbury Cathedral on 26 June 2027.

The Cathedral will be open to the public during the competition.  However, you must be registered to gain access to the Norman Tower and also to purchase drinks from the bar in the Cathedral.

Pre-ordering of goods will help us anticapte demand at the event and speed up registration on the day.  On successful completion of registration, you will be sent a confirmation email, along with attached vouchers for the goods ordered.
