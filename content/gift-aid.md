# Suffolk Guild Gift Aid Administration

The Suffolk Guild of Ringers Gift Aid Administration application is used by authorised administrators to manage Gift Aid declarations for members of the Suffolk Guild of Ringers.

The application supports the recording and administration of Gift Aid declarations, including declarations submitted electronically and declarations received on paper.

Access to the administration application is restricted to authorised users.

This site provides information about the Gift Aid administration application. The application itself is available at:

https://admin.suffolkbells.org.uk/

## Privacy

The Suffolk Guild of Ringers' privacy policy applies to the processing of personal information through this application.

[View the Suffolk Guild of Ringers Privacy Policy](https://www.suffolkbells.org.uk/PrivacyPolicy.php)
